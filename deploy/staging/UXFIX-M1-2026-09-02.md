## What changed, per finding

### 1 · The portal no longer contradicts itself about money and dates
The stale copy was not hardcoded in the views — it arrives as admin-edited free text
(`plexus/settings.key_dates`, the `/api/public/status` detail lines) and outranked `FACTS`. Two
narrow helpers in `facts.js` make `FACTS` win for the facts we actually hold:

- `trueDateFor(label)` — a KEY DATES row whose **label** names a fact we hold is re-dated from
  `FACTS`. Each rule names its subject exactly, so "Plexus Conference & Gala" gets the conference
  range, "Gala seats — €150" gets the gala night, and a row like "Donor Night — during Plexus Week"
  matches nothing and keeps the admin's own date. The ICS export takes the same corrected date, so
  the downloaded calendar and the rail can't diverge.
- `reconcileEarlyBird(text)` — inside a sentence that is about the price, rewrites only the date
  token after through/until/till/before. `"…€150 through 1 Sep"` → `"…€150 through 15 Sep"`;
  a sentence with no price is returned untouched.

`bridges.js`: the August "exact date & venue announced later" rule is **retired** in favour of the
confirmed evening. `COPY.next.soon` and the `PHOTO · <CITY> VENUE — ANNOUNCED SOON` placeholder text
are gone; the card and hero read `Monday, September 21, 2026 · 18:00` and the card now prints
`Waterhouse Room, Gordon Hall — Harvard Medical School`. The old `inWindow` flag (which replaced a
real date with a window label) is now `isNext`, and it selects the FACTS description rather than
suppressing it. Any other city the admin adds is still described exactly as entered.

`home.js`: `CHECK-IN OPENS DEC 4` is derived from `FACTS.plexus.start` instead of typed.

### 2 · The hero CTA now starts the hero's own task
One primary button, state-driven: a member holding nothing gets **`REGISTER — FREE →`** to
`/app/plexus/mine` (the same target the black band's CTA uses); a member holding a ticket gets
`EVENT TICKETS →` to the wallet, plus a real `CHECK IN →` once 4 Dec arrives. `MY MED&X` is deleted
from the row (the avatar, tab bar and drawer already go there), and check-in is a plain micro-label
line until the doors open rather than a status dressed as a button.

### 5 · The stats strip stops greeting members with a row of zeros
A stat renders only once `Number(v) > 0`, so a new member sees no zeros. `MEMBER SINCE` reads
label-first (`MEMBER SINCE 2026`, not `2026 MEMBER SINCE`) and appears only when the year is known.
On day one the strip is the date plus member-since, which is calm and correct.

**Not done here:** dropping POINTS entirely is finding 4 (delete the points economy), which is not in
this batch — POINTS simply hides itself while the balance is 0.

### 6 · Two verbs, everywhere
Defined once, in `facts.js`, so no view can invent a third:

```js
CTA.register            // 'REGISTER — FREE'
CTA.reserve('€150')     // 'RESERVE A SEAT · €150'
```

Swept out of every view: **PRE-REGISTER NOW** (home band, plexus hero, My Plexus hero),
**RSVP · €150** (plexus gala block, gala "why we gather"), **RESERVE YOUR SEAT** (gala hero),
`REGISTER FOR <CITY>` / bare `REGISTER` (bridges hero, card, form). The gala verb always carries the
**live** price (`fmt.eur(D.price.current)` / `D.gala.price`), never a constant. Home's gala status
label `Reserve your seat` became `Seats limited` — a status, now that the CTA beside it carries the
verb. On the home project cards the Plexus and Gala CTAs are normalised onto the two verbs whatever
the status feed sends; the other three cards keep the admin's own label, because they open a project
page rather than the form. Drawer: `Event tickets` → `My wallet` (per finding 14's prescription —
it was a third name for My Med&X sitting in the same list as My Med&X).

### 8 · One settings home
`/app/me` § 03 SETTINGS is now a single link card — *"Account settings live in your Profile &
settings"* → `/app/profile`. The member card, wallet, record, certificates and rewards tabs in
`me.js` are untouched.

**Judgement call, worth reading:** the audit's prescription assumes Profile & settings *keeps*
password. It did not have one — and password, projects-I-follow and research interests existed
**only** on My Med&X. Deleting the section as written would have removed the only editor for three
settings (research interests also feed the profile-completion "Specialty selected" check in
`member.js` and the network suggestions). So all three moved into Profile § 03 ACCOUNT &
PREFERENCES rather than being deleted: a `PASSWORD · CHANGE →` row (same modal, same
`keepSession` semantics), plus `Projects I follow` and `My interests` chip rows with their modals.
They save on the spot through their own routes (they are not part of the profile draft), and toggling
a follow calls `chrome.refresh()` so the FOLLOWING stat appears the moment it turns 1. ~135 lines of
now-unreachable settings code were removed from `me.js`, along with the three API calls it no longer
needs (`/api/notify-topics`, `/api/networking/profile`, `/api/me/locale`).

### 9 · Program & speakers stops being a filtered copy of the overview
Program tab: the ALL/PLEXUS/GALA chips (which could never change a result — every speaker is tagged
`PLEXUS · GALA`) and the "Search speakers…" box over four names are gone, with their state,
handlers and rerender plumbing; the "Click a speaker to view their full bio…" helper line is gone
too, since each card already says `BIO + ADD SESSION →`. The schedule and the speaker grid stay —
that tab is now the canonical roster, and it is where the calendar and PDF already live.

Overview: the four portraits that were a click-away duplicate become one teaser row —
*"4 speakers confirmed for the stage."* — linking into Program & speakers.

**Deliberately not done:** the audit also asks to cut the overview's **program rows** to a teaser.
Left in place. The instruction for this batch says to keep the schedule, those rows are a summary
(day + time range, no session detail) already headed by `FULL PROGRAM →`, and cutting them would
leave the overview with no sense of shape. Flagging it as an open call rather than doing it silently.

### 11 · Featured performers say it once
With no names entered, the chip and the two dark placeholder cards are replaced by a single italic
line under the evening's schedule: *"Two performers confirmed — names announced this autumn."* When
the admin enters real performers (`/api/v2/gala/meta`), the named cards render exactly as before.

I took the delete branch rather than announcing "Tatiana 'Tajči' Cameron & Ante Gelo" on the gala
page: the names are effectively public (the registration form prints them), but announcing a
headline act is Med&X's call to make, not a UX fix. Entering them in the admin portal lights up the
named cards with no further code change.

### 12 · One follow control, not two
`I'M INTERESTED` is deleted from the Plexus hero, from the speakers empty state, and with it the
`interested` handler, the `st.interested` flag and the "Noted" toast. The labelled
`GET UPDATES FROM PLEXUS · ON/OFF` toggle is the survivor because it shows its state. Hero is now
register + schedule, then the toggle.

### 14 · The main menu lists only real doors
`Mentorship` and `Opportunity board` (both `_stub` screens saying "the wiring is on its way") are out
of the drawer. Their **routes stay** in `routes.js`, so direct URLs still resolve — put the links
back when the screens ship.

### 15 · Affordances follow state
- **Gala** — `TRANSFER YOUR SEAT →` renders only when a seat is genuinely held
  (`D.state.key === 'paid'`). The policy line stays: it is what a member reads *before* buying.
- **My Plexus** — the transfer card no longer promises a control to someone with nothing to
  transfer. With no ticket it reads *"Seats are non-refundable — once you hold one, you can transfer
  it to a colleague up to the day of the event."*; with a ticket it says "right from this page", and
  the control is genuinely on that card.
- **My Med&X** — MY RECORD events are deduplicated by event id (falling back to title + date), the
  richest row winning so an *attended* row outranks a merely *registered* duplicate. Attendance
  cards are deduplicated by kind + event, and a card for an event still sitting in the member's
  upcoming list is held back until check-in (year-in-review cards are exempt — they are not
  attendance). This is the "same BUILDING BRIDGES — BOSTON card twice, for an event that hasn't
  happened" defect.

**Skipped, not mine:** the Accelerator's `RESULTS LOOKUP AX26-XXXX` field (shown three months before
applications open) is item 15's third case and lives in `js/views/accelerator.js`, which this batch
is explicitly forbidden to touch. It is untouched and still open.

### Small notes
- **`/signin` 404** — `router.js` learned `redirect` rows and `routes.js` maps `/signin` →
  `/app/auth/signin`, preserving any `?next=`. Done client-side on purpose: Netlify's SPA fallback
  already serves `index.html` for `/signin`, so no `_redirects` change is needed (that file is also
  outside this batch).
- **59 vs 48 members** — Home's band read `/api/public/impact`, which counts every row in `users`;
  Network counts the members you can actually open. Home now prefers
  `/api/v2/network/summary.members` for that stat and falls back to impact, so both screens quote the
  directory number.
- **Truncated mobile placeholder** — a `placeholderShort` ("Name, city, or specialty…") is used
  below 430px via `matchMedia`, instead of the long prompt that clipped to "…a city, 'sle".

## Verification

resolving with a live price; the hero's state branches; the zero-hiding stats strip; me.js keeping
its wallet/card/record while profile.js gained password + follows + interests; the removed chips,

transfer and attendance cards; `/signin`; balanced `<!-- dc: … -->` markers; and the
ink/cream/crimson/gold + Fraunces/Inter language.
