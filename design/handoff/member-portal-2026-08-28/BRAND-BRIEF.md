# Med&X — Brand Brief (for redesigning the Member Portal)

Med&X is a Croatian biomedical non-profit connecting Croatian medicine with the world's
leading institutions (Harvard, Cambridge, Mayo, UCLA…). The brand voice is **institutional,
warm, editorial** — a cultured European foundation, not a tech startup. The reference for
everything is our public site **medx.hr** (screenshots in `site-reference/`).

## What we're redesigning
The **Member Portal** — where guests register for the Plexus Conference and Gala Evening,
hold their QR tickets and wallet passes, apply to the Accelerator, use the Biomedical Forum,
manage their profile. Current-state screenshots provided separately. **Only the visual design
changes — flows, forms, and features stay as they are.**

## Colors (exact, non-negotiable)
- **Ink** `#191512` — near-black, primary dark surfaces and text
- **Cream** `#f7f1e6` — warm paper background (never pure white for page grounds)
- **Crimson** `#9b1b22` — primary action color, accents
- **Gold** `#c9a962` — highlights, premium moments (Gala), hairline flourishes
- Supporting: soft ink `#4a4239` for secondary text; hairlines `rgba(25,21,18,0.16)`

## Typography
- **Display / headlines:** Fraunces (serif, optical sizing; italic for emphasis words)
- **Body / UI:** Inter
- Micro-labels: 11px, uppercase, letter-spacing ~0.18em, weight 600 — used as eyebrows/kickers

## Shapes & texture
- **Rectangular.** Buttons and cards are square-cornered or nearly so (≤6px radius). No pills.
- Buttons: uppercase micro-label text. On our website today: primary = crimson filled,
  secondary = hairline ghost, gold filled for premium moments — treat this as precedent,
  not law; apply the palette as the design calls for.
- Hairline rules (1px) structure sections; generous whitespace; numbered section eyebrows
  ("01 · WHO WE ARE") are a house signature.
- Photography: real event photos (ballroom, conference) — warm, candlelit, never stocky.

## Logo
In `logos/`: `logo.png` (dark, for light grounds) and `logo-white.png` (for dark grounds).
The mark is lowercase "med&X" — the ampersand-X lockup is NOT symmetric; never redraw,
stretch, or recolor it.

## Tone for the portal specifically
Members should feel like invited guests of an institution — think "private members' club
of Croatian biomedicine." Elegant density: informative but never cluttered. The Gala should
feel like the premium jewel of our year; the free Conference open and welcoming — express
that within the palette however the design calls for.

## Don'ts
- No pure white backgrounds, no blue/teal tech palette, no rounded-pill buttons
- No gradients except very subtle ink-depth on dark surfaces
- Don't invent a new logo or wordmark
- Don't invent new features — improve layout, hierarchy and UX freely, but every screen
  keeps its existing features and information
