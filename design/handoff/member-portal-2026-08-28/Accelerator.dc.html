01 THE PROGRAM
A prestigious summer research program placing exceptional Croatian students and early-career researchers at world-renowned labs and clinics. The mission goes beyond the internship: experience an amazing institution, grow professionally and personally, and bring that knowledge home · building lasting bridges in biomedicine between Croatia and the world.

03 HOW SELECTION WORKS
1 Submit your application Online form with your CV, mentor letter, and required documents · opens December 8, 2026.
2 Document review Two-phase document review by the selection committee; shortlisted candidates advance.
3 Interview round Interviews with two Croatian biomedical professionals, scheduled after document review.
4 Selection & onboarding Results arrive by email with your access code · fellowship paperwork begins.
Being selected by Med&X does not guarantee placement · final acceptance is subject to the host institution's approval.
