# The Plexus Awards — public call copy (EN + HR)

For medx.hr and the newsletter. Written to be pasted, not rewritten. House voice: warm, plain,
concrete — dates, who can apply, what to send. No superlatives about ourselves.

Everything below points at **medx.hr/awards** (the portal serves it at `/awards`, and `/awards?lang=hr`
opens the Croatian version). The forms are open **1 October 2026, 09:00** and close
**1 November 2026, 23:59** (Zagreb). Decisions in November. All four awards are presented at the
**Gala Evening, Saturday 5 December 2026, Hotel Esplanade, Zagreb**.

Numbers to keep straight if anything is edited: **four awards · two nominated by the public · one
student fellowship · one chosen by the organizers · up to two laureates each · two Fellows, each
with a funded seat and three minutes on the stage.**

## 1 · Website — the announcement (EN)

### Headline
**The Plexus Awards are open.**

### Standfirst
Four awards, given for the first time at the Med&X Gala Evening on 5 December. Two of them are
decided by nominations — and anyone may write one, including about themselves. One is a fellowship
for students. Nominations open on 1 October and close on 1 November.

### Body

Croatian medicine and science are held up by people most of the country has never heard of: the
surgeon who quietly rebuilt a department, the researcher whose paper is read in six languages and
no Croatian newspaper, the student who talked her way into a laboratory in another country and came
back with something. Once a year we would like to say their names out loud, in a room full of
people who understand what it took.

That is what the Plexus Awards are for. Four of them, presented at the Gala Evening on **Saturday
5 December 2026** at the Esplanade in Zagreb.

**Med&X Lifetime Bridge Award** — for substantial contributions connecting Croatian medicine and
science with the world. A career's worth: the laboratories built, the people trained, the
collaborations that outlasted them. There is no public call for this one. The laureates are chosen
by the organizers and announced on the night.

**Croatian Excellence in Medicine & Science Award** — for a Croatian physician or scientist early
or mid-career, as a rule under forty, whose work already stands up internationally. A body of
research, a clinical programme, a technology, a company: something colleagues elsewhere have
noticed and use. Anyone may nominate, and you may nominate yourself — a self-nomination is read
exactly like any other.

**Rising Talent Award** — for Croatian medical students and young physicians or scientists who went
further than the path in front of them asked. A first paper defended abroad, a project started with
no budget, a summer in a laboratory that had never taken a Croatian student, an idea carried through
the Med&X Accelerator and out the other side. Anyone may nominate, yourself included.

**Plexus Fellowship** — open to high-school students and medical students in Croatia. Bring us a
problem in medicine or health you would like to solve, and a proposal for how you would go at it.
Three short fields, with a word limit on each so that thinking beats padding: **the challenge**
(200 words), **your solution** (300 words), **why you** (100 words). Write in Croatian or in
English — the same panel reads both, and neither is at any advantage. One optional page as a PDF if
a figure says it better. Two Fellows are chosen. Each receives a funded seat at the Gala Evening and
three minutes on the stage to tell everyone in the room what they are working on.

**How a nomination works.** It takes about ten minutes. Tell us who the person is, where they work,
and in no more than 400 words what they did and why it matters. You do not need their permission,
and you do not need to be anybody in particular to write one. If we have their email we write to
them, warmly, to say their name has been put forward — and ask them for nothing.

Every entry is read in full by a small panel, each of whom scores it on three criteria. Decisions
are made in November. Everyone who wrote in hears back from us either way.

**Nominations and applications: medx.hr/awards**
**Open 1 October 2026 · close 1 November 2026 · presented 5 December 2026**

## 2 · Web stranica — objava (HR)

### Naslov
**Plexus nagrade su otvorene.**

### Nadnaslov
Četiri nagrade, prvi put dodijeljene na Med&X Gala večeri 5. prosinca. Dvije se odlučuju
nominacijama — a napisati ih može svatko, uključujući i o sebi. Jedna je stipendija za studente.
Nominacije se otvaraju 1. listopada i zatvaraju 1. studenoga.

### Tekst

Hrvatsku medicinu i znanost drže ljudi za koje većina zemlje nikada nije čula: kirurg koji je u
tišini ponovno izgradio odjel, znanstvenica čiji se rad čita na šest jezika i ni u jednim hrvatskim
novinama, student koja se izborila za mjesto u laboratoriju u drugoj državi i vratila se s nečim.
Jednom godišnje htjeli bismo njihova imena izgovoriti naglas, u sobi punoj ljudi koji razumiju
koliko je to stajalo.

Za to su Plexus nagrade. Četiri, uručuju se na Gala večeri u **subotu 5. prosinca 2026.** u
Esplanadi u Zagrebu.

**Med&X nagrada Most za životno djelo** — za izniman doprinos povezivanju hrvatske medicine i
znanosti sa svijetom. Cijela karijera: izgrađeni laboratoriji, odgojeni ljudi, suradnje koje su ih
nadživjele. Za ovu nagradu nema javnog poziva. Laureate biraju organizatori, a objavljuju se te
večeri.

**Nagrada za hrvatsku izvrsnost u medicini i znanosti** — za hrvatskog liječnika ili znanstvenika
na početku ili u sredini karijere, u pravilu do četrdesete, čiji rad već ima međunarodnu težinu.
Niz istraživanja, klinički program, tehnologija, tvrtka: nešto što su kolege u svijetu primijetile i
koriste. Nominirati može svatko, a možete nominirati i sebe — samonominacija se čita jednako kao i
svaka druga.

**Nagrada za talent u usponu** — za hrvatske studente medicine i mlade liječnike ili znanstvenike
koji su otišli dalje nego što je put pred njima tražio. Prvi rad obranjen u inozemstvu, projekt
pokrenut bez ijedne kune, ljeto u laboratoriju koji nikada prije nije primio studenta iz Hrvatske,
ideja pronesena kroz Med&X Accelerator i izašla s druge strane. Nominirati može svatko, pa i sebe.

**Plexus stipendija** — otvorena učenicima srednjih škola i studentima medicine u Hrvatskoj.
Donesite nam problem u medicini ili zdravstvu koji biste htjeli riješiti i prijedlog kako biste mu
pristupili. Tri kratka polja, sa zadanim brojem riječi, da razmišljanje bude važnije od opsega:
**izazov** (200 riječi), **vaše rješenje** (300 riječi), **zašto vi** (100 riječi). Pišite na
hrvatskom ili engleskom — isti panel čita oboje i nijedan jezik nema prednost. Neobavezno možete
priložiti jednu stranicu u PDF-u ako slika kaže više. Biraju se dva stipendista. Svaki dobiva
plaćeno mjesto na Gala večeri i tri minute na pozornici da svima u sobi kaže na čemu radi.

**Kako izgleda nominacija.** Traje otprilike deset minuta. Recite nam tko je ta osoba, gdje radi i u
najviše 400 riječi što je napravila i zašto je to važno. Ne treba vam njezino dopuštenje i ne morate
biti nitko posebno da biste je napisali. Ako imamo njezinu e-mail adresu, javit ćemo joj se —
srdačno, i nećemo od nje tražiti ništa.

Svaku prijavu u cijelosti čita mali panel, a svaki član ocjenjuje je po tri kriterija. Odluke se
donose u studenome. Svatko tko nam je pisao dobiva odgovor, kakav god bio.

## 4 · Notes for whoever publishes this

- The dates in this file are the ones seeded into the portal (`award_categories.opens_at` /
  `closes_at`). If an organizer moves a deadline in the admin AWARDS tab → SETTINGS, the public page
  follows immediately — **this copy does not.** Change both, or change only the admin and let the
  page speak for itself.
- Do not write "Building Bridges evening". The three events are the Conference, the Gala Evening and
  Building Bridges Zagreb — "all three events" is the house phrasing.
- Croatian diacritics stay (č ć đ š ž). € rather than EUR. No mailto: links in campaign copy.
- The awards page needs no login and no account. Say so wherever there is room: *"No account
  needed — the form is open to anyone."*
