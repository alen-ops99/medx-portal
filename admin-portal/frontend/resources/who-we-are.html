Where it began

Med&X began with a doctor from Split.

He trained in Croatia, found his way into a lab at Harvard, and kept meeting brilliant people back home who never got that door. Med&X exists to build those doors, so the next encounter is a matter of design, not luck.

Who we are

A Croatian biomedical non-profit, bilateral by design.

The exchange runs both ways. Global leaders gain a foothold in a fast-rising biomedical community. Croatian scientists and clinicians gain partners, platforms, and the doors that turn a promising career into a lasting one.

01 — Plexus Conference

Where rising talent meets the people who lead medicine.

Two days of keynotes, panels, and candid conversations in Zagreb each December. Free to attend and deliberately intimate, built so a student can ask a real question and a leader has time to answer it. Across our editions, Plexus has brought Nobel laureates, hospital presidents, and university chancellors to the same room as Croatia's next generation.

02 — Med&X Accelerator

We place Croatian talent inside the world's best labs.

Not exchanges with institutions, placements inside specific labs and clinics at Harvard, Mass General, Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Yale, Columbia, King's College London, and Osaka. Participants return with frontier skills and lasting mentors. Host teams gain dedicated researchers and a durable link to Croatia.
